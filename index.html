Meet the maker

Made slowly.
Worn joyfully.

Hi, I’m Hannah—the hands and happy mess behind Hazy Daisy Jewelry.

Hazy Daisy began as a creative reset: a way to turn quiet moments into color, texture, and something joyful to wear. Now, hundreds of strands later, every design still starts the same way—pulling out beads, following a color story, and seeing where a tiny spark of an idea goes.

I mix polished and playful materials so pieces feel special without feeling too precious. The goal is simple: jewelry that looks like you, makes you smile, and starts conversations.
